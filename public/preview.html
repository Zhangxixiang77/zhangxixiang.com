02 · Problem We Solve

Three structural tensions in basic education

This is not another AI homework tool. It is designed to address long-standing core contradictions that technology has rarely solved well.

01

Uniform pace vs. personalized learning

In a class of 40 students, teachers often teach to the average. Fast learners wait; struggling learners fall behind.

02

Teacher overload vs. student overwork

Preparation, grading and reporting squeeze out time for instructional design and human care.

03

Scattered data vs. intuition-led decisions

Academic, wellbeing and behavior data remain fragmented. Problems are often noticed only after exams.

05 · Core Technology

Claw Constitution

A constitutional layer for multi-agent collaboration: capabilities are routed precisely, long-horizon memory is activated safely, and educational boundaries are enforced continuously.

  • SKILL
    Atomize AI capabilities so teachers can extend them with low-code skills.
  • ROUTING
    Intent + risk routing dynamically schedules specialist agents.
  • MEMORY
    Continuous context across semesters and school years.
  • CONSTITUTION
    Values, boundaries, risks and privacy rules run through the full process.

07 · Campus Scenarios

What the companion looks like in daily school life

The sports coach for healthier routines

“A three-week plan helped me recover my 1000-meter run.”

The confidant for introverted students

With consent, the companion helps teachers notice hidden strengths such as literary sensitivity.
